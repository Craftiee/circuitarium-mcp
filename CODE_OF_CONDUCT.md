# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional community where people can
contribute regardless of background, identity, disability, experience level,
education, or economic circumstances. Technical disagreement is expected;
personal hostility is not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Separate evidence, inference, and opinion.
- Respect privacy, licenses, intellectual property, and project provenance
  rules.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination,
sexualized conduct, trolling, personal or political attacks, sustained
disruption, unwanted private contact, publishing another person's private
information, or pressuring contributors to disclose proprietary material.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code
review, project chat, community events, and when someone represents the project
in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a private
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant.
Actions should be proportional to the conduct, its impact, prior behavior, and
the need to protect the community. Maintainers involved in an incident must
recuse themselves when another maintainer can review it.

## Reporting

Do not publish sensitive incident details in a repository issue. Use the
project's private maintainer contact when one is listed.

> **Release blocker:** A dedicated private conduct-reporting contact has not
> been configured. The maintainer must replace this notice with a real,
> monitored private route before tagging `v0.2.0`. Do not substitute a fake or
> unmonitored placeholder address.

Until that route is configured, use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting
form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) for conduct that violates
GitHub policy. For other project conduct, open an issue containing no incident
details and ask a maintainer to establish a private channel. Maintainers must
not request sensitive details in public. This temporary route is not a
substitute for the required project contact.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical, reviewed promptly and
fairly, and shared only with people needed to investigate or protect those
involved. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in
an investigation is prohibited.
